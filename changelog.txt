26.1.2-4.1.0

BREAKING: The Other has been realigned to its 1.21.1 configuration and will generate differently. Existing
worlds keep the chunks they have already explored, so expect a visible seam where old terrain meets new.

Added
- Teleport pads can be waterlogged

Brought over from 1.21.1
- Allthemodium, vibranium and unobtainium brushes
- Alloy pickaxe
- Ancient, demonic and soul logs can be stripped with an axe again

The Other
- Build height raised from 384 back to 512, infiniburn restored to nether, and permanent night restored
- Travel is 1:1 with the overworld again instead of 12:1
- Lava flows at nether speed again
- Desert, desert hills and soul sand valley generated no ore or vegetation at all - they now carry
  allthemodium ore, vibranium ore and cave vines like the rest of the dimension
- Basalt deltas are sterile again, as they were on 1.21.1
- Coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond and emerald now generate in the deepslate layer

Fixed
- Armour trims (#228)
- Vibranium ore and the whole ancient stone family had no mining tier gate, and copper tools bypassed every
  gate in the mod
- Twelve decorative ancient stone walls, stairs and slabs were unbreakable in survival
- Jade shows the correct harvest tool for ATM blocks again
- Beds no longer explode in the Mining Dimension
- Biome names are translated again (#227)

Translations
- German rewritten in full (Satherov, #232)
- Updated zh_cn (#221)
- Every other language was still keyed to pre-rewrite block ids, leaving roughly half of each file dead
